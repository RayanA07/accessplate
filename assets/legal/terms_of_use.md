# AccessPlate Terms of Use

Last updated: June 4, 2026

## Not medical care

AccessPlate provides food-access decision support. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose or treat disease, and is not a substitute for advice from a physician, registered dietitian, allergist, or emergency services.

Do not rely on AccessPlate for severe allergy reactions, medication interactions, or urgent medical decisions.

## Data realism

Store matches, travel estimates, and bundled ZIP access models are approximations. Inventory, hours, prices, and benefit eligibility can change. Verify critical purchases in person when possible.

## Acceptable use

You may use AccessPlate for personal decision support and non-commercial research demos tied to the AccessPlate project. Do not misrepresent prototype outputs as verified clinical guidance.

## Limitation of liability

AccessPlate is provided as a prototype without warranty. The development team is not liable for food choices, purchases, travel decisions, or health outcomes made using the app.
