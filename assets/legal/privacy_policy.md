# AccessPlate Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

AccessPlate is designed to keep your food-access profile on your device.

## What stays on your device

- Name and onboarding preferences
- Allergens, medical filters, budget, pantry items, and location settings you enter
- Logged meals and daily tracking history
- Bundled food catalog cached in local storage

## What may leave your device

- Optional live nearby-store lookup through OpenStreetMap public services when you are online
- Optional grocery product lookup when Kroger API credentials are configured in a build

AccessPlate does not require an account, does not sell personal data, and does not include ad or analytics SDKs in the current release.

## Children

AccessPlate is not directed to children under 13 without guardian involvement.

For competition, research, or support questions, contact your team captain or faculty sponsor listed in the DEBUT submission materials.
