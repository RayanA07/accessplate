# AccessPlate Evidence Scorecard

Use this file to summarize small-sample evidence from demo scenarios, user interviews, and stakeholder reviews in one place.

## Recommended summary metrics

Track these for each tested scenario:

- time to identify first stop
- time to identify buy first item
- percent of users who identified use-from-home correctly
- percent of users who identified skip-first correctly
- percent of users who identified the backup correctly
- average realism score
- notes about benefits confusion
- notes about language or literacy confusion

## DEBUT-oriented evidence prompts

### Significance

- Does the scenario show a real barrier in low-resource food access?
- Is the barrier more specific than "healthy eating is hard"?

### Impact

- Did the app reduce confusion or wasted steps?
- Did it improve a decision that matters on the same day?

### Innovation

- What deterministic access-aware behavior is visible here?
- What would a generic recommender likely miss?

### Prototype strength

- Is the logic visible in the actual product?
- Can the scenario be reproduced reliably in the app?

## Strong early numbers

These are not formal targets, but good internal signs:

- most users can identify first stop within `30 to 45 seconds`
- most users can identify buy first within `45 seconds`
- the Spanish/plain-language scenario performs similarly to English on action-finding tasks
- stakeholder realism scores average `4/5` or better on pantry, source, and benefits logic

## Important warning

Do not present these metrics as clinical outcomes or population-level proof. Present them as prototype usability, realism, and decision-support evidence.
