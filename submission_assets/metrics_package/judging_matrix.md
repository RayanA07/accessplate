# DEBUT Judging Matrix

Use this file to tie the live demo and metrics package back to the NIH NIBIB DEBUT 2026 judging story.

## Significance of the problem

- Scenario evidence:
  - `01_emergency_no_cook_45211.md`
  - `02_pantry_stretch_19133.md`
  - `05_spanish_plain_language_60623.md`
- What to emphasize:
  - food access is constrained by travel, pantry reliability, benefits, and cooking setup
  - users are often deciding under same-day pressure, not optimizing wellness in a vacuum

## Impact on users / care

- Scenario evidence:
  - `02_pantry_stretch_19133.md`
  - `03_snap_tight_budget_77026.md`
  - `04_wic_grocery_vs_convenience_90011.md`
- What to emphasize:
  - the app gives immediate action: where to go first, what to use from home, what to buy first, and what to skip
  - the engine can reduce wasted trips and poor-fit purchases

## Innovation

- Scenario evidence:
  - `01_access_burden_case_45211.md`
  - `03_benefits_case_77026_90011.md`
- What to emphasize:
  - deterministic source-trip planning
  - pantry depletion and restock logic
  - ZIP/state-aware access and benefits reasoning
  - bilingual plain-language output without a chatbot

## Prototype strength

- Scenario evidence:
  - `04_spanish_plain_language_case_60623.md`
  - all five linked demo scenarios
- What to emphasize:
  - the logic is implemented in the prototype, not only described conceptually
  - the repo includes demo, metrics, validation, and support-letter scaffolding
  - the app runs offline-first and keeps the core profile local
