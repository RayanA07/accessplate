- Best first stop: convenience store or dollar store before a longer grocery attempt

## What the presenter should show

1. Emergency mode enabled in the profile

- `Significance`: shows the app addressing same-day food insecurity, not generic wellness.

- `Innovation`: deterministic emergency routing with access burden and purchase order.
- `Prototype strength`: the action path is visible on-screen, not just described in docs.
