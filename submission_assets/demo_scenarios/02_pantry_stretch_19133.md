# Demo Scenario 02: Pantry-Stretch Restock Day

- Bundled ZIP snapshot: `19133` Fairhill, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania
- Main judging theme: use-from-home planning plus minimal add-ons

## User snapshot

- Transportation: `walk`
- Travel limit: `15 min`
- Prep setup: `microwave`
- Benefits: SNAP
- Emergency mode: off
- Language: English
- Plain-language mode: on

## Pantry on hand

- Have enough: `rice`
- Running low: `beans`
- Need restock: `oats`

## Real-world pressure today

- Budget: under `$12`
- Situation: trying to cover tonight plus the next meal with one small trip
- Sources available: food pantry, dollar store, grocery store

## Expected app behavior

- Best first stop: pantry or dollar store before a harder grocery trip
- Use from home first: `rice`
- Buy first: one or two staple add-ons that stretch what is already at home
- Skip first if budget gets tight: pricier foods that do not improve meal coverage
- Backup: pantry-friendly basic from the next-best source

## What the presenter should show

1. Pantry status chips: enough / running low / restock
2. Meal basket card with pantry support
3. Today plan checkpoints: `Now`, `Next meal`, `After that`
4. Explanation detail showing `No purchase needed` versus real buys

## Why this helps the submission

- `Significance`: matches how low-resource households stretch pantry staples.
- `Impact`: reduces unnecessary spending by centering food already at home.
- `Innovation`: deterministic pantry depletion and restock logic.
- `Prototype strength`: the basket and today plan stay aligned with pantry state.
