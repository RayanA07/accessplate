## App name
AccessPlate

## Short description (80 chars)
Offline food decisions for real budgets, travel limits, and store access.

## Full description
AccessPlate helps people in low-resource U.S. settings answer one practical question: what is the safest, cheapest meal I can actually get today from places I can reach?

Unlike generic nutrition apps, AccessPlate models pantry food, SNAP/WIC context, transportation burden, cooking limits, emergency days, and nearby store realism. It gives a clear action plan: where to go first, what to use from home, what to buy first, and what to skip.

Key features:
- Works offline after setup
- Deterministic, explainable recommendations
- Bilingual English/Spanish support
- Emergency quick setup for same-day pressure
- No account required; profile stays on device
