- [x] one-paragraph innovation framing drafted in `submission_narrative_packet.md`
- [x] one slide or document summarizing model boundaries drafted in `model_boundary_summary.md`

## What not to overclaim

- live neighborhood inventory across all source types
- state-perfect WIC item eligibility
- clinical outcomes without external study data
- broad generalizability without more field testing
