## Tasks

1. Ask the participant to explain what they think the app is for.
2. Give one scenario and ask them to find where to go first.
3. Ask them to identify what to use from home first.
4. Ask them to identify what to buy first and what to skip if money runs short.
5. Ask them to compare the top pick with the backup.
6. If relevant, switch to Spanish or plain-language mode and repeat the key task.

## Questions

- What part was easiest to understand?
- What part was confusing?
- Did the benefits wording make sense?
- Did the app feel realistic for your day?
- What would make you trust it more?

## Findings

- Could the participant find the first stop?
- Could the participant find the buy order?
- Could the participant explain the backup?
- Did large text / readability / tap targets cause trouble?
- Did any words feel too technical?
